Authentication

Every call to the REST API needs a token.

Send each request over HTTPS with a JWT in the Authorization header. Your IdP issues the token after SSO, and it expires after one hour.

Service accounts

Back-end jobs use a service account instead of SSO. Keep its key in your secrets manager, and rotate it at least every 90 days to stay inside our SLA.

Tokens are cached at the CDN edge for 60 seconds. Revoke one from the dashboard to clear it at once.
